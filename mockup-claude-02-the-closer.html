The Closer

← index

A phone-shaped screen you open once a day, in the evening. It never shows a chart you have to interpret. Every row is a specific number you could still hit tonight and what hitting it buys — sorted by how little it costs. Distance-to-threshold, not progress-to-date.

Thursday, Sep 10

Urgent

these get worse, not just later

Cheapest wins left today

ranked by minutes required

Already banked today

and what it moved

Your usual active window

so "no time left" is checkable

Coming up

thresholds you cross soon
