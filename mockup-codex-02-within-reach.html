The pitch · choosing invitations without creating chores

This is the best eventual home dashboard. It finds small distances to meaningful thresholds and offers at most three or four choices. Pin activities you want to see. Rotate ties so quick wins do not always displace difficult projects.

Do not sum unlike activities into a productivity score. Do not turn every pastime into a deadline. A milestone can simply recognize effort already given.

Minutes, qualified active days, and completion counts mostly use existing logs. Reading position and book length are proposals. A “since tracking began” label prevents incomplete imports from masquerading as lifetime history.

The paper palette is deliberate: this should feel like an invitation you can leave on a desk. Large typography and HTML progress bars serve proximity better than another chart. ECharts belongs in the drill-down, not every card.
