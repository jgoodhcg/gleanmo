The pitch · routine semantics and honest streaks

Use weekly consistency for workouts and creative practice. Daily streaks demand perfect calendars; weekly targets give you more than one path to success.

Meditation can use a daily streak if you choose it. Never combine its streak with brushing, reading, and exercise into one fragile number.

Habit logs already contain sets of habit IDs. A routine needs a name, required habit IDs, a timezone, and a day-boundary policy. Match each habit at most once per routine day across logs. All three must be present for a complete night; partial nights remain partial.

For this concept, the winddown day ends at 04:00 America/Detroit. A 00:30 Friday log belongs to Thursday night. Workout and meditation use local calendar days. Historical logs can be evaluated, but a missing log is not proof the activity was skipped.

Future days never count as misses. An open week never breaks a completed-week streak. Paused weeks are labeled and excluded from eligible-week ratios; they do not silently become successes.
