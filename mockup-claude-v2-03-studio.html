The model behind this page

Activity on the session, default from the project. project-log/activity-id says what you did; project-log/project-id says what it served. A new session copies project/activity-id as its default. Garage Build is editing most days and writing some.

Backfill once, conservatively. Sessions before the field existed were backfilled only when their project has a single activity (Low Tide → writing, Sketchbook → drawing). Multi-activity projects stay unclassified and show grey. Changing a project's default later never rewrites history.

Deliverables are tasks. task/project-id, task/state and task/done-at already exist; the only addition is an optional task/milestone to separate "draft chapter 7" from "buy printer ink". No percent-complete, ever: three of four milestones is a count, not 75% of a book.

Qualifying days. An intention like "4 drawing days" needs a floor, stored on the goal (goal/min-per-day). Two 10-minute sessions on one day still make one day, and it still doesn't qualify.
