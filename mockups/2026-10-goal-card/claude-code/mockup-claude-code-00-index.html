Round 2: 04 · Ruler, generic — 03 reworked as a general rule, with five goal shapes to test it on. Round 3: 05 · Ruler, quiet — less ink, let-binding key numbers, phone and desktop side by side. Round 4: 06 · List and goal — goals list, one page per goal, pinned goals in the sidebar.

From the 2026-10-05 dogfood notes in roadmap/090-goal-live-today.md and the mobile item in 081. Same Pullup goal as the screenshot: 245 of 5,000 reps, 19 elapsed days, 347 days left. The controls above drive every frame below. Open "Details · chart" in any frame to see what moved out of the way.

  • Keep pace = today's share at the required rate (the shipped "needed today"): 13.7 → 14.
  • Gap = how far behind the even-pace line you were at the start of today: 259.6 − 245 ≈ 15. Fixed for the day.
  • Closes the gap = reps still needed today to be on the even-pace line by midnight: 273.2 − logged ≈ 29 at the start of the day.
  • Thresholds: "round step" is a round number near 3 days at the required rate (50 here); "even-pace line" uses the gap-closed point (260); "both" shows whichever comes first, plus the other.
  • Common to all three: a compact goal select replaces the table above the card (pinned goals show as chips on wider screens), one number per rate, "elapsed" in place of "completed", a Log button, and the chart and secondary numbers behind a disclosure.
