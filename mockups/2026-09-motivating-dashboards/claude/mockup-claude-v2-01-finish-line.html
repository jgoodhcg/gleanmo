Gear = needed ÷ your rate, both per eligible opportunity. 1.00× means keep doing exactly this. It is the one number that compares reps, minutes, books and nights directly — so it's the sort key.

The arithmetic, and the rules that keep it honest
  • Opportunities = eligible days in the goal window. Pull-ups and workout sessions count Mon · Tue · Thu · Sat; writing counts Mon–Sat; everything else counts every day.
  • Your rate = done ÷ opportunities elapsed (today included). Needed = remaining ÷ opportunities after today, so the slider reads "what tomorrow asks of you".
  • Vs. even pace is expressed in opportunities, not units: "2 training days behind" is a thing you can picture fixing; "−98 reps" isn't.
  • Past the target, needed shows 0 and the excess is kept and shown. Never divide by zero on the last day; show met or the gap.
  • A goal starts where capture is trustworthy. The pull-up goal starts Mar 2 on purpose. Workout sessions start Jan 1 to show the other option — a visibly marked floor.
