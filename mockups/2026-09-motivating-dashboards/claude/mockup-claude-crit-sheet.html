    After the wall

    What is the first real dashboard?

    All four rounds landed in the same place: one goal with a finish line, on data you know is complete, before anything else. They disagree on which goal comes first and on how much generality to build underneath it.

    Claude v1
    Pace Board, with the goal entity and the daily series engine under it. Next, the ghost diverging bar on the overview.
    Claude v2
    Finish Line on one goal, meditation minutes, slider included. If a week of it doesn't pull, stop and rethink.
    Codex v1
    Pace lab for one goal with complete capture: meditation minutes, or a pull-up goal starting today.
    Codex v2
    A saved practice collection plus a fixed-period goal with project and habit adapters, then the pace board on top.

    Worth answering at the end of your notes:

    1. Which goal comes first: which practice, what unit, over what window?
    2. Which plates must be on screen one?
    3. What's good but can wait until the basic loop works?
    4. What should never show up?
    5. Which schema additions are you willing to make: goal, activity-id, routine, page-count, milestone?
