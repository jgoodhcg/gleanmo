Why this page looks like this

It opens on a time budget rather than a ranking because "what's cheapest" is the wrong question at 9:52pm — "what fits" is the right one, and only you know the answer. The default comes from your own hour-of-day history.

Cards are capped and sorted by distance to a threshold, not by cost. Deep work is pinned to tomorrow rather than competing with ten-minute wins it would always lose to.

The "can honestly wait" section exists because a screen that only ever asks for more becomes a screen you stop opening. When a week's intention is met, the page says so and stops.

Data: pull-ups, drawing, writing, music and meditation work from existing logs plus a goal. The book card needs book/page-count and reading-log/page-position (marked proposed). Winddown needs a routine definition.
