The Ledger

— numbers you have gone past ← index

The other half of the spreadsheet feeling. Pace charts pull you forward; this page is the one you read when you are already tired and want evidence that it added up. Almost no charts on purpose — a list of facts wants type, not boxes.

Lifetime

Every log you have ever written, including the Airtable years. These only ever go up. That is the entire point of the number.

Crossed

Thresholds you went past this year, newest first. Derived, not stored — the crossing date is just the first day the running total exceeded the threshold.

Next up

The same list, one step forward. Ordered by how soon you get there at your current rate.

Trophy timeline

Every crossing this year on one axis. Dense clusters are months where several practices compounded at once; the July gap is visible without being told about it.

Records

Personal bests, all derived from the same interval logs. Gold means it was set in the last 60 days — a record you are currently near is worth more than one from 2023.
