    Rate: your average since the goal started, per day (per week for day counts). Needed: what each remaining day needs, from tomorrow on. Needed ÷ rate: 1.00× means your current rate finishes on Dec 31; ▲ means it falls short. Vs even pace: days ahead of or behind an equal share every day. Tick on a bar: where even pace puts you today.

    About this mockup

    Built from the crit-sheet feedback: A4's layout, A11 and A13's table, the A2/A3 burn-up without the slider, and F1's last-year line. Every number comes from one daily series per goal.

    • Rate = total ÷ days elapsed (255). Needed = remaining ÷ days left (110).
    • Vs even pace = (total − target × 255 / 365) ÷ (target / 365), in days.
    • Next threshold: step = target ÷ 10, rounded to 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ (10,000 reps → 1,000; 105 h → 10 h; 190 days → 20).
    • The 2025 line shows only when the goal's source has complete history from Jan 1, 2025. That is one "complete since" date per source, kept in code.
    • Left off this screen on purpose: the tonight slider, "tonight" cards, generated sentences, streaks and the practice grid (next step), activity, routine and milestone fields.
    • Colors are app tokens. Cyan = logged, violet = needed from tomorrow, grey = even pace and 2025. Amber ▲ and green ✓ mark needed above or within your rate. Palette check on #161b22: colour-blind and normal-vision separation pass; the lightness-band check fails because the app's neon tokens are brighter than the reference band.
