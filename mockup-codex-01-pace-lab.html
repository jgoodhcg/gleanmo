Why this works · data and arithmetic

Build this first. It reproduces your proven loop: accumulated effort, a reachable threshold, and a required pace you can change today.

The baseline is the end of September 9: 252 elapsed days, 113 remaining. Average = 6,940 / 252. Required = 3,060 / 113. Original pace = 10,000 / 365. Ahead = 6,940 − 10,000 × 252 / 365, rounded to 36. After today, required = max(0, 3,060 − today’s reps) / 112. Projection = 6,940 + 33 × 113.

Existing exercise lines provide reps. Join line → set → session and filter the chosen exercise IDs. The goal, dates, timezone, and eligible schedule are new configuration. Imported history must be complete for this period; otherwise label totals “since tracking began.” Do not invent a lifetime total from incomplete exercise imports.

Use ECharts lines, markLine, and tooltips in a future implementation. This self-contained mockup uses inline SVG so it works offline.
